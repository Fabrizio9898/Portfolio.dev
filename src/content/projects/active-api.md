## What it does

It's the backend for [Active](/proyectos/active): it handles authentication, users, sports centers, courts, bookings and payments.

## Under the hood

- NestJS modular architecture with TypeORM on PostgreSQL.
- Stripe payments (checkout and webhooks).
- Full route, DTO and entity documentation with Swagger.
